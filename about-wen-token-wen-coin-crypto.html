● What does "wen" mean in US slang?

In US slang, "wen" is not a word on its own; it is best known from the crypto phrase "wen moon", which asks "when moon?" — when will a coin's price shoot up. It is a playful misspelling of "when" that expresses the wish for a crypto's value to rise sharply, much like "to the moon". ● What does "wen" mean in Chinese? "Wen" (pronounced differently depending on the character and context) can mean "to ask" (问, wèn), "culture" or "writing" (文, wén), "warm" (温, wēn) or "to hear" (闻, wén), and can also refer to other things, such as "multicolored clouds" (雯, wén) — or, in English, a skin cyst called a "wen". The meaning depends on the specific character, its tone and the context.

Common meanings of Wen (wén, wèn, wēn): 问 (wèn), said with a falling tone, means "to ask" — for example, "请问" (qǐngwèn) means "May I ask?". 文 (wén), said with a level tone, refers to culture, language, writing or literature, and is also the root of the surname Wen. 温 (wēn) means "warm", "mild" or "to warm up", and is also a common surname. 闻 (wén), said with a level tone, means "to hear", "news" or "famous".

Other meanings: 雯 (wén) refers to multicolored clouds. Wen (noun): in English, a "wen" is an old word for a sebaceous cyst, a lump on the skin, unrelated to the Chinese characters with the same sound.
