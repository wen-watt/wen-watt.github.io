What do you know about Wen?

A quick quiz about the Wen cat, the $WEN coin and the Solana tools around it. Ten random questions from a growing base of 250 checked facts — from silly meme trivia to the things that actually keep your wallet safe. No levels, no sign-up: just answer, learn and see how high you can score.

How it works

  • 10 questions per round, picked at random: some fun, some basics, some tech and one about safety or the wider world.
  • 4 options, 1 correct. Click an answer or press 1–4 (or A–D).
  • 100 points for each right answer, plus a speed bonus of up to 50 — it drops by 5 every second you think.
  • Missed one? The right answer lights up with a short knowledge card and a link to read more on this site.
  • At the end you get your score, a Wen rank and a list of what to revisit. Play again for a fresh mix.
