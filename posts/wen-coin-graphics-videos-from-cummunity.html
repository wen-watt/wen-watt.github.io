In July 2025 the community opened its archive: folders of phone backgrounds, videos, GIFs and art, free to download and reuse. The original post presented the folders together with an automatic market analysis and an invented "search index" table. We removed both. What remains is the useful part — a map of the open material and how to use it well.

Open material was prepared by the community, to prove the openness and involvement of the power of $WEN.

How to use the material

  • Share and remix freely — that is what the folders are for.
  • Credit the community when you can, and keep the cat kind.
  • Never attach a buy link or a price promise to community art; it turns culture into an advert.
  • Point to the real token by its address, not by name — see the trust chain.

"The coin for the people"

The original's overview called WEN "the coin for the people" and listed its roots: Meow's poem fractionalized into one trillion tokens, an airdrop to more than a million wallets, cat accounts on X owned by the community figures differ, plush toys and events. That part holds; for the details, see what Wen built beyond the chart. One fix: the share burned after the airdrop was just over 27%, not "about 30%".

What we removed

  • An automatic "analysis" with prices, scenarios, a "1% probability" and investing advice.
  • A "US technical matrix" of meme themes with percentages that had no source at all.
  • Links to price trackers and placeholder links that led nowhere.

The folders are the story. Everything else was decoration.
