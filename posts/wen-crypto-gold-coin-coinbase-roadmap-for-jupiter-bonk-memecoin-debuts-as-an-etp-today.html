Jupiter (JUP) on Solana: Coinbase listing status

As of the latest data on December 3, 2025, the Jupiter (JUP) token is the governance and utility token of Jupiter, a leading decentralized exchange (DEX) aggregator on the Solana blockchain. It is not yet fully listed for spot trading on Coinbase's main exchange, but today brings an important update.

JUP at a glance:

  • Overview: Jupiter finds the best token swaps across the Solana ecosystem, with low-slippage trades, limit orders, perpetuals and more. JUP holders take part in governance through DAO votes.
  • Market stats (as of today):
  • Price: roughly $0.55–$0.65 USD (volatile; check live sources for the exact figure).
  • Market cap: about $1.7–$2 billion USD.
  • Circulating supply: ~3.1 billion JUP (of 10 billion total).
  • All-time high: ~$2.00 (January 2024).

Coinbase listing update:

  • Current status: JUP is not yet open for direct spot trading or custody on Coinbase's centralized exchange. But on December 3, 2025 (today), Jupiter (JUP) was officially added to Coinbase's Asset Listing Roadmap. That points to a strong chance of a future listing, since a roadmap entry often comes before full integration (for example, pairs such as JUP/USD).
  • Coinbase already tracks the JUP price on its price page (coinbase.com/price/jupiter), but only for information—no buying or selling yet.
  • Earlier Coinbase involvement:
  • In May 2024, Coinbase International launched JUP perpetual futures (up to 20x leverage), available worldwide (except U.S. retail users).
  • Note: do not confuse it with an unrelated Ethereum "Jupiter" token (same JUP ticker, but a privacy/encryption project). Coinbase briefly supported an ERC-20 version in 2022–2024, which led to user complaints about transfers and a delisting in 2024. That is not the Solana JUP—keep them apart.
  • Why it matters: a full spot listing could lift liquidity and price (past examples: Solana tokens such as PYTH and JTO rose 20–50% after a Coinbase listing). No exact date has been announced, but roadmap entries often turn into listings within 1–6 months. Watch the Coinbase blog or X (@coinbase) for news.

Price reaction today and the $Jup crypto roadmap:

The roadmap news has lifted sentiment, with JUP up ~5–10% over the past 24 hours amid wider Solana ecosystem excitement. Community threads on X and Reddit are upbeat, but expect swings around token unlocks (for example, Jupiter's ongoing vesting schedule).

Institutional Milestones and Core Protocol Hardening

The different paths of $BONK and $WEN show how two separate strategies can strengthen one layer-1 blockchain at the same time:

  • Institutional integration ($BONK): Regulated tracking products on traditional financial venues, such as the BONK ETP debut on Switzerland's SIX exchange, validate the asset class with traditional brokers without any need for direct crypto custody.
  • On-chain optimization ($WEN): Instead of aiming at legacy investment products, $WEN serves as the founding proof of concept for the Wen New Standard (WNS), bringing structured, gas-efficient NFT frameworks.
  • Shared network resilience: Together these high-volume assets have repeatedly absorbed peak traffic, directly hardening Solana's network baseline for the major decentralized exchanges.
