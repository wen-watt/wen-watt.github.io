Two news items, recorded

  • A regulated BONK product in Switzerland. In late November 2025, Bitcoin Capital launched an exchange-traded product tracking BONK on the SIX Swiss Exchange — a dog memecoin reaching ordinary brokerage accounts (FXStreet).
  • Jupiter's token on a listing roadmap. On December 2, 2025, a large U.S. exchange added JUP to its public listing roadmap reported (report). A roadmap is a notice, not a listing.

What they mean — and do not

  • Memes are reaching traditional finance. The BONK product follows the same path as the Solana funds of October 2025 — see BSOL on Wall Street.
  • None of it concerns WEN directly. Neither item involves the Wen token; they show the neighbourhood growing.
  • Exchange news is not a reason to buy anything. This almanac records events and points to no trading venue; for calm habits, see a market day, read calmly.
