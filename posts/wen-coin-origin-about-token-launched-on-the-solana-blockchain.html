Small corrections

  • The poem's title is "A Love Letter to Wen Bros", not "to the Wen Brothers".
  • WEN did not "launch using WNS": WEN is a regular Solana token, and WNS is an NFT standard introduced alongside it (the WNS 0.0 proposal).
  • WNS was proposed by a coalition starting with Ovols and Jupiter, supported by the Wen Foundation — not developed by the Foundation alone.
  • "The first community coin based on a fractional NFT" is how its creators described it as described.
