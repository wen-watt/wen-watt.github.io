The legend of Christmas cats, and how it links to the Wen meme cat

The post tells a folk-style legend in which cats have long been the quiet keepers of holiday warmth: they kept mice away from winter food stores, appeared on Victorian Christmas cards as signs of good luck, and turn up across many cultures — from Japan's Maneki-neko to European tales of cats leading travelers home through the snow. It ends on a theme the site often comes back to: cats as a small, steady source of comfort and luck, loosely connected here to the $Wen brand.
