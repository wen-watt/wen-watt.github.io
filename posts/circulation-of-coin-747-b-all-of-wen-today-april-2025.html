Three supply words

TermMeaning
Total supplyAll tokens that exist on-chain right now
Circulating supplyThe part a tracking site considers freely movable — each site decides this its own way
BurnedTokens destroyed for good; they no longer count in the total

Only the first is a hard on-chain fact. The second is an estimate made by a third party.

WEN's supply, step by step

  1. One trillion — Meow's poem split into 1,000,000,000,000 tokens (January 2024).
  2. About 70% for the community — set aside for the airdrop to more than a million wallets.
  3. More than 271 billion burned — the unclaimed part, destroyed on January 29, 2024, over 27% of supply (Decrypt).
  4. Roughly 729 billion left — simple arithmetic: one trillion minus the burn.

Where "747 billion" fits

The figure of 747 billion does not match the arithmetic above figures differ. Tracking sites differ in what they count and how quickly they update, and a number copied from one of them in April 2025 can drift from the chain. That is exactly why the original's last line was right: do not trust a round number — look it up at the source. A fixed supply alone also says nothing about value; read more than a meme for what really matters in a token.

Check it yourself on-chain

  1. Open the WEN mint on the Solana Explorer.
  2. Read the supply field — that is the total that exists today.
  3. Look at the mint authority: if no one holds it, no new tokens can ever be created.
  4. Compare with any tracker you like — and note the difference.
