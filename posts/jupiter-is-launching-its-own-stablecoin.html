JupUSD: Jupiter's Own Stablecoin, Built with Ethena

October 8, 2025

Historical post This post records events as they were reported at the time. Facts are checked against sources; nothing here is a forecast.

On October 8, 2025, Jupiter — the biggest swap aggregator on Solana and the platform where Wen was born — announced its own dollar stablecoin, JupUSD, built with Ethena Labs. Three months later it went live. This post explains what JupUSD is, how it is backed, where it is used, and what it does and does not mean for the Wen cat. No price targets, no "merger" rumours — just the mechanism.

What JupUSD is

  • A dollar stablecoin native to Solana, designed as the "house money" of the Jupiter ecosystem.
  • Built with Ethena Labs, the team behind the USDe and USDtb dollar tokens.
  • Announced on October 8, 2025 for the fourth quarter; Jupiter began rolling it out on January 5, 2026.

How it is backed

At the announcement, Jupiter said JupUSD would start fully backed by USDtb — a token issued by Ethena that holds mainly BlackRock's tokenized treasury fund BUIDL — with the option to add USDe over time. At launch in January 2026, about 90% of reserves were in USDtb, with USDC making up the rest, and Ethena handling day-to-day reserve operations.

The original post also said JupUSD would be "overcollateralized with Solana liquid staking tokens". That does not match the official announcement, so we have corrected it figures differ.

Where it is used

  • Jupiter Lend: deposits mint a reward-earning token called jlJupUSD.
  • Jupiter Perps: collateral for perpetuals; Jupiter planned to move a large part of the USDC in its liquidity pool into JupUSD — about $750 million at the announcement, about $500 million in the launch plan figures differ.
  • Trading tools and the mobile app: limit orders, dollar-cost averaging and a single balance across products.
  • Partners: a pairing token on Meteora and other Solana apps.

Why a house stablecoin

Stablecoins are the everyday money of crypto: they let people trade, lend and send value without the price swings of the assets around them. When an ecosystem issues its own, it keeps more of that activity — and its economics — at home, and it can design the coin to work smoothly across all its products. It is the same logic as Western Union's USDPT on Solana a few weeks later: own the rails you use every day.

For ordinary users the practical question is simpler: what backs the coin, who manages the reserves, and where can it be redeemed? Those are the questions to ask of any stablecoin, JupUSD included.

What it means for Wen

The original post called Jupiter "Wen's son" and JupUSD a boost for "father Wen". It is a fun image with a true core: Wen was the first token on Jupiter's launchpad, and its launch was the rehearsal for $JUP. But a stablecoin does not buy, back or burn WEN. What JupUSD does for the Wen community is indirect: a stronger, simpler Solana money layer makes every app on the chain — including the free payment models in Wen Open Lab — easier to use.

The Lab's roadmap already points that way: its next model is "pay in WEN, receive a stablecoin".
