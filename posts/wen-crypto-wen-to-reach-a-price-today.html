On October 8–9, 2025, data sites reported a strong one-day rise for WEN together with a much higher trading volume than usual. How to read a price day

  1. Separate the move from the reason. A chart shows that something happened, rarely why. Most explanations are written after the fact.
  2. Look at the time frame. A large daily percentage can be a small step on a monthly or yearly chart.
  3. Volume is attention, not approval. More trading means more people noticed — buyers and sellers alike.
  4. Check the token, not the ticker. Several assets share the name WEN — even a burger chain's stock. Only the Solana address tells you which one you are looking at.
  5. Wait a day. Excitement fades quickly; knowledge does not.

About price forecasts

The original post quoted an automated forecasting site that predicted a short-term rally. Such forecasts are produced by simple models from past prices; they are not research and they are often wrong. We have removed the forecast. If a page promises where a meme coin will be in two weeks, the safest assumption is that nobody knows — including the page.

Fear and greed, explained

The post also mentioned a "fear and greed index". These indexes combine signals such as volatility, momentum and social activity into one number from "extreme fear" to "extreme greed". They describe the mood of the market, not the value of any project — useful as a weather report, useless as a compass.

What stays when the chart is quiet

  • A fair start: more than a million wallets received WEN, with no private sale — a benchmark for Solana launches.
  • An open standard: WNS, readable by anyone, used by outside builders within weeks.
  • Open tools: free models in Wen Open Lab.
  • A culture: art, stories, events and a cat people like — collected in Wen Media and the image stories.

None of those move with the chart. That is why they are what this almanac writes about.

Five safety habits for busy days

  1. Never share a seed phrase or private key — no real project, exchange or event will ask for it.
  2. Ignore direct messages offering "help", "recovery" or "early access".
  3. Check the token address against the official site before any transaction.
  4. Be careful with links in replies under popular posts; copies of real sites appear quickly.
  5. Decide calmly. Nothing that is real disappears in an hour.
