The Landmark Shinhan–Solana Agreement (August 21, 2026)

On August 21, 2026, the financial heavyweight Shinhan Asset Management, a firm with about $96.6 billion in assets under management, formally chose the public Solana blockchain as the infrastructure for its new RWA (real-world asset) product.

A four-party memorandum of understanding (MOU) was signed by Shinhan Asset Management, the Solana Foundation, the fintech platform Etherfuse and the decentralized exchange Orca.

Scope of the project: launching a tokenized ultra-short-term bond fund in Korean won (KRW) for foreign institutional investors. The product follows the structure of BlackRock's BUIDL, the biggest player in the global RWA market.

Why Token-2022 Matters: Compliance Built In

For a regulated Korean bank, an open public blockchain brings strict AML/KYC duties. This pilot was possible precisely because of the Token-2022 extensions.

  • In the proof-of-concept phase, the whole issuance-to-distribution chain, including automatic investor screening at the protocol level, ran on Token-2022.
  • The bank used built-in Transfer Hooks to verify the identity of fund-unit buyers instantly, so no unauthorized investor could get the tokenized won outside Korea's compliance rules.

Korea's Regulatory Backdrop in August 2026

August brought tighter rules and legal groundwork ahead of the bigger 2027 reforms:

  • On August 20, 2026, strict amendments to South Korea's Special Financial Information Act came into force, sharply raising the bar for registering and auditing Virtual Asset Service Providers (VASPs), including mandatory checks on major shareholders.
  • These stricter rules pushed commercial banks such as Shinhan toward compliance solutions written into the code itself (exactly what Token-2022 offers), so the technology could be proven before February 2027, when Korea's new Security Token Offering (STO) law takes full effect.

The Bank of Korea Gets Ready for the September CBDC Mega-Pilot

At the same time, all through August the Bank of Korea (BOK) was finishing the infrastructure for Phase 2 of Project Hangang, the state's wholesale CBDC and tokenized-deposit pilot.

On August 11, 2026, central bank officials told the media the system was ready to expand testing to 9 commercial banks and 500,000 users.

The state pilot starts in September 2026, while Shinhan Bank's commercial project on Solana grows alongside it, creating a distinct two-track landscape in South Korea: the state builds a closed wholesale CBDC, while commercial banks test the RWA market on fast public networks with Token-2022.

Where $WEN Comes In: a Direct and an Indirect Link

The link between the $WEN memecoin, the Bank of Korea's roadmap, the wholesale CBDC rollout and Shinhan Bank's Solana project has both a direct technical side and an indirect reputational and economic side. WEN served as an early, large-scale test that showed financial institutions Solana's public ledger was safe.

Direct link: a shared technical base

  • WEN as a pioneering standard (WNS): the Wen Foundation, built by the Jupiter team, created WNS (Wen New Standard), a light, low-cost layer built directly on Token-2022.
  • A ready codebase for RWA: when Shinhan Asset Management, with Etherfuse and Orca, launched the tokenized KRW fund pilot in August 2026, they used the same Token-2022 extensions (above all Transfer Hooks for compliance and Transfer Fees) whose stability under millions of transactions was first shown during WEN's airdrop and trading.
  • Orca's infrastructure proven: in the Korean RWA pilot, the decentralized exchange Orca acts as liquidity provider. It was on Orca, in January–February 2024, that the first million-scale liquidity pools for Token-2022 assets were tested, with WEN as the main asset. Korean banks thus took over a mechanism already stress-tested in extreme market conditions.

Indirect link: proof for regulators and institutions

  • An enterprise-grade stress test: before WEN launched, major financial institutions, including Korean regulators FSC and FSS, were wary of Solana because of its history of outages. WEN's January 2024 launch sent tokens to more than 1,000,000 wallets at once. The success of that experiment told the Bank of Korea and commercial banks the network could carry national-scale load.
  • Making Token-2022 credible: in the eyes of institutions, the WEN memecoin moved Token-2022 from "experimental developer draft" to "industrial-grade standard". Without that proof, Shinhan Bank would have been far less likely to risk its reputation and billions in assets by choosing Solana to tokenize the won in 2026.
  • A two-track development model: the success of the Token-2022 ecosystem, beginning with WEN, set a precedent that Korean economists call "parallel development". While the Bank of Korea builds a closed, tightly controlled wholesale CBDC (Project Hangang), commercial banks became confident that the open Solana network could serve a retail RWA market at the same time, thanks to compliance built into the code.

왜 Token-2022가 중요한가 -- 설계 단계부터 컴플라이언스를 내장

규제를 받는 한국 은행이 공개 블록체인을 사용하려면 엄격한 AML/KYC 요건을 충족해야 합니다. 이번 파일럿이 가능했던 것은 전적으로 Token-2022 확장 기능 덕분입니다.

  • 개념증명(PoC) 단계에서 발행부터 유통까지의 전 과정 -- 프로토콜 레벨의 자동 투자자 심사를 포함 -- 이 Token-2022를 통해 구현되었습니다.
  • 은행은 내장된 전송 후크(Transfer Hooks)를 활용해 펀드 지분 구매자의 신원을 즉시 확인함으로써, 한국의 컴플라이언스 규정을 우회한 미승인 투자자가 토큰화된 원화를 취득할 수 없도록 했습니다.
