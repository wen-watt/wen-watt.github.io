Mapping the money graph in the open.

IQ.Wiki launches Ecosystem Map — an interactive, non-commercial graph that puts every verified relationship around a wiki in plain view. First up: the Wen network.

Most crypto research is still scattered in pieces — a founder named in one article, an exchange listing hidden in another, a chain dependency no one ever draws out. IQ.Wiki, the community-run, non-commercial Web3 encyclopedia, has closed that gap with Ecosystem Map: a live, interactive graph attached to each wiki, now running on the Wen page.

Ecosystem Map — Wen's 16 verified links, grouped by category

01 A Map, Not a Sales Funnel

The most important design choice is what Ecosystem Map does not do: it does not rank sponsors, sell placements or steer users to any exchange or token. As a non-commercial public resource, IQ.Wiki has no reason to tilt the graph — every node is there because it is documented, not because someone paid for it. That difference turns a visualization into a research tool.

02 Centralized Markets and Decentralized Rails in One View

Wen's graph shows in one place what used to take five open tabs to check: a decentralized memecoin, a decentralized host chain and a centralized-adjacent exchange all sit on the same map, with the exact relationship between them labeled.

03 How Openness Builds Trust

In a market where due diligence often feels like a chore, a free, sourced relationship map that is always on is a quiet but real upgrade to public trust infrastructure. There is no paywall and no account needed. The idea is simple: verifiability is the product.

The bottom line: Ecosystem Map does not tell users what to think of a project — it shows exactly how the pieces fit together and leaves the decision to them.

04 What It Means for the Future of the Market

As on-chain activity grows across chains, exchanges and founders, seeing relationships at a glance — instead of piecing them together from scattered sources — becomes core research infrastructure rather than a bonus. By launching Ecosystem Map on Wen first and expanding it wiki by wiki, IQ.Wiki is working toward a fully cross-referenced, community-verified map of the whole crypto industry: useful for researchers today and a foundation for whoever builds the next generation of Web3 tools.
