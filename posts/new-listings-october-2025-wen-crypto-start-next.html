Culture and community

Most of the list is culture: art, parties, contests, plush toys, a fitness event, cat accounts with millions of followers. None of it requires anyone to buy anything, and all of it brings people closer to the story. That is the same road the community kept walking afterwards — a football tournament, a charity plush, a story world.

Tools and technology

The technical items are fewer but lasting: a validator and its staking token, domain names, community rewards, an AI experiment — all built on Solana, all usable without trading. They sit beside the deepest technical contribution of all, the Wen New Standard, and beside this almanac's own answer: free, tested models in Wen Open Lab.

What the list shows

The community post ended with a jab at doubters: if someone really knew what was coming, why would they still be here? The better answer is the list itself. Projects that only trade leave charts. Projects that build leave things people can use, wear, play with and learn from. Wen's list is long on the second kind — and that is what an almanac should record.
