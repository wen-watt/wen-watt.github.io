What "a real PPP model" means

In crypto slang, PvP ("player versus player") describes markets where one trader's gain is another's loss. The Wen community uses PPP for the opposite mood: people making things for each other — videos, art, explainers — where everyone who joins adds something and nobody has to lose community term. The original blog explained both terms in a separate post: PPP and PvP in crypto.

Free, voluntary community work is the healthiest signal a project can have — far more telling than any weekend chart.

An analysis without data

The removed text began: "without specific price data from reliable sources… I'll analyze this surge based on general principles". That is worth remembering as a lesson. An analysis that has no data is a story, however confident it sounds — and much of what circulates about small coins is exactly that. Before trusting any market write-up, ask: where do the numbers come from, and can I check them? Our guides help: on-chain numbers, explained and a market day, read calmly.

Two facts that hold

  • Most of WEN went to the community. About 70% of the supply was set aside for the airdrop to more than a million Solana wallets.
  • The unclaimed part was burned. When the claim window closed on January 29, 2024, more than 271 billion WEN — over 27% of the total supply, nearly 39% of the airdrop allocation — were burned for good (Decrypt).
