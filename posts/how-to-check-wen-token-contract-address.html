Names can be copied, addresses can't. New tokens with "Wen" in their name appear from time to time, sometimes on other chains. They may look friendly, but a name proves nothing. On a blockchain only one thing cannot be copied: the token's address. Here is the real one, and a two-minute habit to check any token.

The one Wen ($WEN) address

Wen lives on Solana. Its one token mint is:

WENWENvqqNya429ubCdR81ZmD69brwQaaBYY6p3LCpk

It is the address shown by the official Wen site, the official X account and the Solana Explorer. Independent listings such as CoinMarketCap show the same mint; the link is on the sources page. Use listings to cross-check the address, never for prices or promises.

Three checks before you hold any token

  1. Address from two official sources. Take it from the project's own site and from its official account, and compare them character by character (at least the first five and the last five).
  2. It is a real token mint. Open the address in a block explorer. On Solana a mint is owned by SPL Token or Token-2022; a wallet or anything else is not a token.
  3. Its powers. Read who can still create, freeze or move tokens. The Lab explains each power in plain words: ten walkthroughs and the trust chain.

Same name, different token

A token on another chain with "Wen" in its name, for example Wen Cat on Robinhood Chain, is a separate project. It has a different address, often in a different format (addresses starting with 0x belong to Ethereum-style chains, not to Solana), and it is not related to Wen on Solana. This is not an accusation against anyone: it is simply how names work on open chains. The answer is always the same: check the address.

Questions people ask

What is the official Wen ($WEN) contract address?

Wen lives on Solana. Its one token mint is WENWENvqqNya429ubCdR81ZmD69brwQaaBYY6p3LCpk. The official Wen site, the official X account and the Solana Explorer all show this same address.

Is a token called Wen on another chain the same as Wen?

No. Anyone can give a token the same or a similar name. A token on another chain, for example Wen Cat on Robinhood Chain, is a separate project with its own address and is not related to Wen on Solana.

How do I verify any token contract in two minutes?

Take the address from two independent official sources and compare it character by character, open it in a block explorer to confirm it is a token mint, then read who can still mint, freeze or move tokens.

Can a token name or logo be protected on a blockchain?

No. Names, tickers and logos are only metadata and can be copied freely. The mint address is the only part that cannot be copied.
