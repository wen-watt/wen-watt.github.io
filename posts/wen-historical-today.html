The summary, fact-checked

The summary saidVerdict
"An all-time high on May 14, 2023"Impossible. WEN did not exist until January 26, 2024.
"70% of tokens airdropped to over 1 million wallets"Consistent with the record. The airdrop reached more than a million Solana wallets.
"Speculation about a possible burn of unclaimed tokens"Already happened — on January 29, 2024 more than 271 billion WEN, over 27% of supply, were burned (Decrypt).
"Traded on Uniswap on Ethereum and Polygon"Warning sign. WEN is a Solana token. Anything called "WEN" on another chain is a bridged copy or a different token with the same ticker.
"Two different ranks on two listings"Same-ticker confusion — several unrelated tokens share the name.
"A governance token for WEN DAO"Reported · official X — check the official X account for its current status.

Five habits for reading any summary

  1. Check dates against the launch. Anything "before the beginning" is an error.
  2. Check the chain. A Solana token is identified by one Solana mint: WENWENvqqNya429ubCdR81ZmD69brwQaaBYY6p3LCpk.
  3. Prefer events to opinions. A burn on a given day is a fact; "undervalued" is a mood.
  4. Ignore precise-looking numbers without a source. Two decimals do not make a figure true.
  5. Ask whether it was written by a machine. Automatic summaries mix sources and years with total confidence.
