What "alt season" means

"Alt season" is a period when alternative coins — everything other than Bitcoin — rise faster than Bitcoin itself. People watch Bitcoin dominance, Bitcoin's share of the whole crypto market: when it falls quickly, money is flowing into other coins. The best-known alt seasons came at the end of 2017 and in early 2021; both ended sharply. "Cat season", in the MEW post, is the same idea told as a fable.

Seasons are names for what already happened. Nobody can schedule the next one, which is why "who knows" was the most honest line in the original.

Chart words, explained

TermMeaning
Trend lineA line drawn through past highs or lows to show a direction
BreakoutThe price crossing such a line
RetestThe price returning to the line it crossed
EMA (9, 21 weeks)An average of past prices that gives more weight to recent ones
InvalidationThe level at which the analyst admits the idea was wrong
RankA coin's position by market value — it moves every hour

What chart analysis cannot do

  • Every line is drawn by a person. Two analysts draw two different trend lines on the same chart.
  • "Rules" are habits, not laws. "Breakout plus retest equals bullish" is true until it is not.
  • Past rallies do not repeat on schedule. A rise after one pattern in 2024 says nothing certain about 2025.
  • The most useful line is invalidation. It is the one place where an analysis admits its own limits.
