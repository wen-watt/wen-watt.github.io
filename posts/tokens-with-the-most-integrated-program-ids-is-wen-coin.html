In September 2025 a chart went around the Solana community: tokens with the most integrated program IDs. WEN was in the top ten, beside staking tokens and an oracle. Unlike a price chart, this one measures something lasting — how many on-chain programs are built to work with a token. This post records the chart, explains what it measures, and corrects the bigger claims that grew around it.

What a program ID is

On Solana, every on-chain program — a swap, a lending market, a staking pool, a launchpad, a game — has an address called its program ID. When a program is written to accept, hold or move a particular token, that token is "integrated" into it. Counting the distinct programs that touch a token gives a rough picture of how widely it is woven into the network.

What the number means

  • It measures building, not buying. Each integration is a program someone chose to write or configure for the token.
  • It favours older, useful tokens. Stablecoins lead because nearly everything handles dollars.
  • It does not measure quality. Ten small programs count the same as one large one.
  • For WEN it reflects its launch. As the first LFG token and a public stress test, WEN was integrated early by swaps, wallets and tools across the ecosystem.

Claims, checked

  • "345+ integrations" — matches the chart chart only.
  • "More than a third of all projects in the world" — not what the chart says. WEN's 345 is about a third of USDC's 974, and close to JitoSOL, PYTH and mSOL. That is impressive for a meme; it is not a third of the world.
  • Elevate Media partnership, June 12, 2025 — announced; see Wen and Elevate Pictures. The list of Elevate's own past clients is Elevate's claim, not a Wen partnership unverified.

"Energy coin": a community idea

The original called WEN a future "energy coin" — a new type beyond memecoins, connected to everything opinion. Read soberly, the idea has a measurable core: a token becomes infrastructure when many independent programs choose to work with it. That is what this chart counts — and it is the same path WNS and Wen Open Lab follow: be useful to builders first.
