Drop 001: Wen x Bonk — the Teaser Before the Plush

October 25, 2025

Historical post This post records events as they were reported at the time. Facts are checked against sources; nothing here is a forecast.

Four days before the drop, almost nothing was known. Playground posted a date — October 29, 2025, 12 PM Eastern — a title — "Drop 001: Wen x Bonk" — and a mood: mystery. The communities filled the gap with guesses, emojis and one strange line, "going swimming". This post captures that teaser moment, explains who the two mascots are, and separates what the cat and the dog really do together from what the timeline only hoped for.

The teaser

  • What: Drop 001: Wen x Bonk — the first numbered drop on Playground.
  • When: October 29, 2025, 12 PM Eastern time.
  • Details: deliberately secret. The announcement promised something "out of this world" and nothing more.
  • Where to follow: the Bonk post on X that started the countdown.

Teasers like this are a small art form. They give a community something to do together before anything exists: decode the emojis, argue about the format, draw fan art. By the time the drop opens, thousands of people already feel part of it.

Who Playground is

Playground is a platform for limited collectible drops. It tends to present lifestyle-style art and to work with other brands and communities, as in its earlier "Shrouded Playground" series reported. Choosing Wen and Bonk for its very first numbered drop was a statement: start with the two characters that Solana users recognise instantly.

Why Solana for collectibles at all? Because minting and moving digital items there costs a fraction of a cent and settles in seconds. That makes small, playful drops practical — the same reason the Wen New Standard was designed as a lightweight way to build NFTs on Token-2022.

The cat and the dog

Wen and Bonk are often called the cat-and-dog duo of Solana. Their stories are different, which is exactly why they fit together:

A correction to the original post: Wen did not launch "in late 2024" — its airdrop was in January 2024. And the cat account Wen acquired on X, according to a 2025 newsletter report, is @TweetsOfCats, not the account the original named.

What they do together

The friendship is visible in public: shared posters and spaces, a team spot in the Copa de los Memes football tournament, and — as it turned out — a plush toy whose proceeds went to an animal charity. That is the kind of cooperation anyone can check: it happened on stage, on X and in shops.

Two communities that cheer for each other also bring each other new people. A Bonk fan who meets the Wen cat at a drop or a match may go on to read about the poem, the airdrop and the WNS standard — and the other way round.

What the drop turned out to be

The secret did not last long. On October 29 the drop opened as a limited Wen x Bonk plush, made with Toymak3rs, with all proceeds going to the animal charity Buddies for Paws. The full story is in our follow-up: Wen x Bonk Plush: Drop 001 with Playground and Toymak3rs.
