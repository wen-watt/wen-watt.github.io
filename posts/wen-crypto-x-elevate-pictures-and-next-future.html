Wen x Elevate Pictures: Meet the Team, October 16, 2025

October 10, 2025

Historical post This post records events as they were reported at the time. Facts are checked against sources; nothing here is a forecast.

Before a story world can be built, people need to meet the storytellers. On October 10, 2025, Wen announced an open audio Space for October 16 at 12 PM Eastern time: a chance for the community to meet the team at Elevate Pictures and get a first look at what comes next for the Wen cat. This post is the announcement, the line-up, the videos the community made around it — and a link to the full story of the partnership.

The Space

  • What: "Meet the Team" — an open audio Space on X with the Elevate Pictures team.
  • When: Thursday, October 16, 2025, 12 PM Eastern time.
  • Why: to connect the community with the studio and give a sneak peek at what is next for Wen.
  • Announcement: Wen on X.
