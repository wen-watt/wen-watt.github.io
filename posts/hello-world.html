The board

The community's Pinterest board collects Wen art: space cats, phone wallpapers, posters for events and songs. It sits beside the other open archives — the open folders, the media kit, the almanac's own Wen Media and the Pinterest page of this site.

Cat tales · WenIn Russian and other Slavic villages, a cat was the first to step into a new house. Folk belief held that the cat brings peace to the home and takes any misfortune upon itself — so the family followed it in.

Why pictures work

  • No jargon. A picture of a cat in a space suit explains "community" faster than any white paper.
  • No price. Pinterest is a place for taste, not trading — a calm entrance for people who will never read a chart.
  • Shareable. Wallpapers and posters travel to places crypto posts never reach.
