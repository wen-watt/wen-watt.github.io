A bit of history
On November 19, 2025, the Solana DeFi ecosystem gets a long-awaited jolt as Jupiter Exchange hosts its 60th Planetary Call—the recurring community audio and video series that has become a pillar for updates, alpha drops and candid banter across the "Jupiverse." This edition stands out not only for its milestone number but for the triumphant return of @weremeow, the mysterious "cat" behind Jupiter's rapid rise. Promoted as a catch-up on Jupiverse news, a deep dive into the Catlumpur vision and early hints of a mysterious "special thing" planned for 2026, the call (streamed live on YouTube via Luma) sets out to reignite $JUP momentum in a choppy market.

Weremeow: the wow behind $Jup and $Wen

Weremeow (also called Meow, Twitter: @weremeow) is the pseudonymous founder of Jupiter Exchange, a leading DEX aggregator on Solana, and the creator of the $WEN meme coin (a community token built on a fractionalized NFT of his poem "A Love Letter to Wen Bros").

Weremeow and Catlumpurr

He keeps steering Jupiter's growth into a "DeFi superapp," hosting events such as CatLumpurr and focusing on bringing the world into decentralized finance.

Weremeow came to crypto several years ago, working on Ethereum projects such as Kyber and WBTC and advising Blockfolio, before turning to Solana around 2021.

  • He founded Jupiter in October 2021 as a liquidity aggregator offering the best swap rates on Solana, and it soon became the most popular trading interface in the ecosystem.
  • Known for his pseudonymous "cat" persona, he favors a chaotic-neutral style and often speaks philosophically about crypto, community and money.
  • In early 2024 he created $WEN as a cultural meme coin, splitting his poem into a trillion tokens and airdropping them to more than a million wallets as both a test and a tribute to "wen" culture.
  • In 2024 Weremeow launched Jupiter's $JUP governance token with one of the largest airdrops in crypto, putting community alignment and decentralization first.
  • He has spoken openly about Solana's strengths for real-world crypto uses such as payments and social transactions.
  • The $JUP launch drew controversy, including over token sale mechanics and airdrop farming; he addressed it openly and admitted some mistakes.
  • In 2025 he publicly reflected on errors such as trying too hard to please the community in DAO decisions, and proposed tokenomics changes such as supply cuts.
  • Weremeow puts product quality, community consensus and long-term vision ahead of short-term hype, and sees tokens as a reflection of a project's value.

Catlumpurr, Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia, Jan 31 to Feb 1, 2026: video about the event

Here is just a small part of what may be discussed at Catlumpurr in Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia, Jan 31 to Feb 1, 2026, with a video about the event

CatLumpurr is Jupiter Exchange's community-first summit, organized by founder Weremeow in Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia, from January 30 to February 1, 2026. Unlike a classic conference, it centers on participation through an unconference format in which attendees shape the sessions, discussions and feedback on Jupiter's future. Day 1 brings major product announcements and updates from the Jupiter team on projects such as the DeFi SuperApp and Jupnet. Before the main program there are social activities—yoga, games, pickleball, gym sessions and community meetups—to help people connect. It closes with an after-party with networking and collaboration chances for builders, $JUP holders and Solana fans, plus travel subsidies for selected attendees. Organized by Weremeow, CatLumpurr celebrates Jupiter's community roots and echoes the broad, inclusive spirit of the $WEN Crypto airdrop to more than a million wallets. The event shows Weremeow's experimental approach, much as $WEN tested new mechanisms such as the WNS standard ahead of major launches. $WEN stood for "wen" culture and community anticipation, themes CatLumpurr nurtures through participatory unconference sessions on Jupiter's future. Just as $WEN fractionalized a poem NFT to open up ownership, CatLumpurr aims to open up DeFi development to attendees' input. Its look back at past lessons draws on experiences such as the $WEN launch, stressing fair distribution and long-term community alignment.

How Jupiter $JUP came to be, and why $WEN Crypto is its father, by co-founder Weremeow

  1. The $WEN token was built as a precursor experiment to test the mechanisms for the coming $JUP launch.
  2. $WEN came from fractionalizing an NFT of Weremeow's poem with the new Wen New Standard (WNS).
  3. WNS is a light NFT standard built on Token-2022 for stronger composability and lower costs.
  4. WNS makes NFT creation simple by fixing supply at 1 token with 0 decimals.
  5. The poem NFT was minted through WNS and then split into 1 trillion fungible $WEN tokens.
  6. $WEN acted as the proof of concept for WNS fractionalization.
  7. The huge $WEN airdrop to more than 1 million wallets stress-tested Jupiter's LFG Launchpad.
  8. That launchpad test made sure the infrastructure ran smoothly for the later $JUP distribution.
  9. $WEN showed that a fair, broad community airdrop is possible without favoring whales.
  10. What was learned from $WEN's WNS rollout and launch shaped the community-first creation of $JUP directly.

How WNS and Wen Crypto Were Born: a Meow-tastic Solana Story
Follow along: X: https://x.com/JupiterExchange 🔗: https://jup.ag/

In the playful world of Solana DeFi, January 2024 saw the birth of the Wen New Standard (WNS), a light, open-source NFT protocol imagined by @weremeow—the pseudonymous "cat" co-founder of Jupiter Exchange (WNS, Wen New Standard, Wen crypto, Jup). Built on Solana's Token2022 extensions, WNS puts ecosystem composability, flexibility and backward compatibility first, letting communities launch NFTs with very little overhead. It all began with Meow's viral poem, a cheeky "wen moon?" ode to crypto impatience, minted as the very first WNS NFT—then fractionalized for shared ownership and instant meme magic.
