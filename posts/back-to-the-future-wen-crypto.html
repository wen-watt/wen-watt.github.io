Back to the Future: a Fan's Note on the Wen Cat, Read a Year Later

October 14, 2025

Historical post This post records events as they were reported at the time. Facts are checked against sources; nothing here is a forecast.

On October 15, 2025, a fan of the Wen cat left a short note on the old blog. It described the "Wen phenomenon", admitted that many people saw it as a joke, and dared to imagine Wen as something much bigger by 2026. It ended with five words: Let's leave a note. Meet me in the future. A year later, the almanac opens that note again — not to judge the dream, but to see which parts of it people actually built.

The note, in its own words

The Wen phenomenon is the rise of a cat-themed meme coin that captured crypto and internet culture — sassy cat memes, "wen moon" catchphrases and a hyper-engaged community that turned a joke into a cultural force. Let's leave a note. Meet me in the future. — October 15, 2025, a fan

We keep the spirit and leave out the parts that were never facts: the original mentioned "celebrity endorsements" and "play-to-earn games" without sources unverified, and spoke about market capitalisation. This almanac does not publish market talk.

Why skeptics shrug

The fan was honest about the doubts. Traditional investors dismiss meme coins as fads; next to Bitcoin they look like a niche internet joke, and "meme-driven hype without clear utility" is the most common criticism. That criticism deserves a fair answer, not an angry one — and the best answer is to show what the community actually does.

What the fan hoped for

  • That culture — cats, memes, art — would keep bringing new people in.
  • That the community model would grow into real use, not only trading.
  • That practical tools would arrive: integrations, applications, ways to pay.
  • That Wen might one day work as an everyday digital currency.

Cat tales · WenIn old Siam, villagers carried a cat in a basket through the streets in the Hae Nang Maew ceremony, asking for rain for the rice fields; the Tamra Maew, the Cat-Book Poems, described cats that bring their owners good fortune.

What is still a dream

Wen as a widely accepted everyday currency did not arrive by 2026 — and the almanac will not pretend it did opinion. What did arrive is more modest and more durable: open tools, honest documentation and a culture people enjoy. Dreams like the fan's are allowed; they are just marked as dreams. If you want to add your own note to the future, share it with the community on X — and come back next October.
