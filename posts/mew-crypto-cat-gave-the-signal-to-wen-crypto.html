Who MEW is

MEW ("cat in a dogs world") is a Solana memecoin launched in March 2024. Its lore is a small fable: a cat named MEW standing up to a world ruled by dog coins. It grew very quickly after launch reported and became one of the best-known cat tokens. On October 6, 2025 its account posted a short "summoning cat season" message — MEW on X — which community artists turned into the poster above.

What "cat season" means

For years the memecoin world was dominated by dogs — Dogecoin, Shiba Inu, Bonk, WIF. "Cat season" is the community's name for the moment cats got their turn: MEW, Popcat, and the Wen cat born from Meow's poem. It is a cultural mood, not a market law. Cats and dogs are friends on Solana too — Wen and Bonk released a plush together for charity and played in the same football tournament.

Cats and people: 9,500 years

  • Cyprus, about 9,500 years ago: a cat buried next to a person at Shillourokambos is one of the earliest signs of cats living with humans.
  • Ancient Egypt: cats guarded the grain and were linked to the goddess Bastet — read Wen and the cats of ancient Egypt.
  • Norse myth: the goddess Freyja rides a chariot pulled by two cats.
  • Folklore everywhere: cats appear in charms, omens and stories — some lucky, some dark. The original post mentioned a cruel old Scottish rite; we leave such rites in the history books where they belong.
  • The internet: from early cat photos to today's memes, cats became the web's favourite characters — the culture Wen grew from.

Cats cheering for cats

The nicest thing about the MEW post is its tone: one community greeting another. That is the healthiest side of memecoin culture — shared jokes, shared art, shared events — and it costs nobody anything.
