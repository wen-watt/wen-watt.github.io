Who Speaks Here: Editorial, Data and Cookie Policy

There is no star author on this site, no pedestal and no badge. The voice you read is Wen: the cat speaking for the community that keeps this almanac. This page explains how that voice gathers its material, how to check it, and what the site does and does not do with your data.

1. One voice: Wen, for the community

Every post is signed by Wen. Behind it stands a community of holders, builders and curious people who collect, sort and retell what is happening around the $WEN token, the WNS standard and the Solana ecosystem. The almanac began on WordPress and is being moved, post by post, to this open GitHub repository, where every change is public. Search engines see the same thing: the structured data on each page names Wen as the author and the Wen Almanac as the publisher.

2. Open data collection

  • Public sources only. Everything here comes from openly available material: official project documentation, public GitHub repositories, regulator releases and filings, exchange and explorer pages, press coverage and public posts on social networks.
  • Sources stay visible. Wherever a fact matters, the link to its source sits next to it, so you can open the original in one click.
  • No private information. Nothing insider, confidential or non-public is used, and nothing is bought or scraped from behind a login.
  • Free to reuse. Community graphics and media are shared for open use; our own image archive lives in Wen Media.

3. A subjective model you can check

Collecting facts is the easy part; connecting them is where opinion begins. When the almanac links events together, ranks strengths and weaknesses or sketches a roadmap, that is a subjective model: one community's reading of public data. We say so openly, and we mark the places where a link between events is an interpretation rather than a confirmed fact (see, for example, the caveats in WNS, Token-2022 and the Shinhan deal and South Korea, CBDC and tokenization).

The model is meant to be tested, not trusted blindly:

  1. Open the linked source and compare it with what we wrote.
  2. Check token contracts only through authoritative explorers and indexers, such as CoinMarketCap and the Solana Explorer.
  3. Read the code yourself: the Wen Program Library is public, and our Wen Program Library review shows where to start.
  4. If something is wrong or out of date, tell the community on X (@wenwencoin) or through the repository, and the page will be corrected.

4. Not financial advice

The almanac is educational. Nothing here is investment, financial, legal or tax advice, and nothing here is an offer to buy or sell any asset. Prices and figures are snapshots from the date of writing and may have changed. Decisions are yours, made on your own research.

5. Privacy

This is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages. It has no accounts, no forms, no comments and no newsletter, so it does not collect personal information from you. The site itself runs no analytics and no advertising trackers. GitHub, as the host, may keep standard technical server logs (such as IP addresses) for security and operation, under GitHub's privacy statement.

6. Cookies

  • Our own cookies: none. The site sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser.
  • Embedded videos: YouTube videos are embedded through the privacy-enhanced youtube-nocookie.com domain, which does not store cookies until you press play. Once you play a video, YouTube's own policies apply.
  • Fonts: page fonts load from Google Fonts, which receives a standard request from your browser.
  • Outbound links: sites we link to (exchanges, explorers, social networks, news outlets) have their own privacy and cookie policies.

7. Changes

When the way the almanac works changes, this page changes with it, and the history of every edit stays visible in the public repository.
