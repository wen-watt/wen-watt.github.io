Compare WEN with any Solana token

Names, tickers and logos can be copied. The powers written into a token cannot hide. Pick a token or paste any mint address: this page reads WEN and that token straight from Solana and lines up what the chain says. Who can still create tokens, freeze a holder, move anyone's tokens or take a fee on every transfer. No prices, no rankings, no wallet.

    How the procedure works

    1. Your browser sends one read-only request, getAccountInfo, for the WEN mint and one for the token you chose. Nothing is signed, no wallet is connected.
    2. The answer comes back decoded by the RPC node (jsonParsed): program, supply, authorities and Token-2022 extensions.
    3. Each power is turned into plain words: mint and freeze authority, transfer fee, permanent delegate, transfer hook and who can change it, accounts frozen by default, non-transferable, close and pause authority.
    4. The two columns are lined up; rows that differ are tinted, keys that can still act are marked. The link with ?mint= lets anyone repeat the same check.

    Read it fairly

    A key that can act is not good or bad by itself. Stablecoins and regulated assets often must keep a freeze authority or a permanent delegate by law; a meme coin usually gives these keys up. The page compares powers, it does not judge projects. Preset addresses are common community references: the page always reads the chain, so check that the address is the one you mean. The mint itself is only part of the picture; programs behind a transfer hook need their own review.
