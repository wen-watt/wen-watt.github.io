Setup is locked

Only the owner of this device sets the receiving wallets, so nobody at the till can put their own wallet in. Forgot the PIN? Reinstall the app on this device, enter the receiving wallets again and set a new PIN.

More receiving wallets (optional)

One counter can take payments into several wallets: departments (furniture, toys, garden) or branches. Add them here; at the till you choose where each sale goes. Every wallet is checked on-chain and stays receive-only.

Each wallet gets a number, a type and a name. For example:

  • #1 · Main · Shop
  • #2 · Department · Furniture
  • #3 · Branch · Downtown
  • #4 · Tips · Bar tips
  • #5 · Online · Web orders

The ledger adds them up per wallet (head office view); the CSV carries the number, type, name and address.

Receive-only by design

The counter only knows your public address. It never connects to your wallet, never asks it to sign and holds no key, so nothing outside your wallet can send your money. Refunds and transfers are your decision, made inside your wallet app.

Taxes, receipts and your accountant

The counter does not declare anything and is not a fiscal device: it is a new, direct link between a buyer who pays by phone with a QR and you who receive. Before you rely on it, show it to your accountant or finance lead: say that you want to receive payments to a wallet and declare them. If your rules allow it, keep the wallet's statements for each period, declare the income and show receipts and refunds. Many countries do not forbid it, but the declaration procedure is yours to check. A useful tip, not legal or tax advice.

If your accountant says yes, scale it: you hold a modern, transparent and free way to accept payments.

Safety and law, in one picture

The Solana network is the Sun; wallets are planets that answer only to their owners; this counter is a telescope that watches payments arrive and writes them down. It never holds funds. Ownership, refunds, records and taxes are handled on your wallet's side, on your phone or desktop. The counter is free and takes nothing; you are responsible for your business and taxes under your country's rules.

Growing? Give each counter its own till wallet. The manager can refund buyers from it during the day and move the day's takings to your company wallet (for example a Squads multisig) every evening. The counter stays receive-only.
