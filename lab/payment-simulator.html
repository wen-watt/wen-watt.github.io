Models become useful when they meet real numbers. Enter your shop's volume, card terms and chain costs, and the simulator compares the monthly cost and the money waiting for settlement. Every formula is shown, nothing is sent anywhere: the calculation runs in your browser.

The formulas

  • Revenue = orders × average order.
  • Card payment fees = revenue × card fee % + orders × fixed fee.
  • Chargeback costs = orders × chargeback rate × (average order + fee per chargeback).
  • On-chain fees = orders × network fee in SOL × SOL price + revenue × conversion %.
  • Money waiting for settlement = revenue ÷ 30 × settlement days.

What the simulator leaves out

  • Setup, integration, compliance and licensing costs.
  • How many of your customers already hold a Solana wallet.
  • The buyer-protection value of chargebacks, which is real for customers.
  • Price movement of non-stable tokens between payment and conversion.

The point is not that one rail always wins. The point is to replace opinions with numbers you can check, and then test the winner on devnet with Stage 4.

Where Wen fits in this model

If a shop accepts WEN as one payment option, volatility matters: set the conversion field to your real conversion cost and remember that the value of a WEN payment can move between receipt and conversion. Stable pricing belongs to stablecoins; WEN brings community and attention to the checkout.

Open use policy

  • Free and open. All Lab code is released under the Apache License 2.0, the same license as the Wen Program Library. Use it, change it, ship it, commercially or not; keep the license notice.
  • Open tools only. Built on public, documented tools: Solana SPL Token-2022 (Token Extensions), the token layout of the Wen New Standard (WNS), Solana Pay, and LiteSVM for local testing.
  • The rule: always verify your own model. Run the tests, start on devnet, read the official documentation, review every account and amount, and get an independent audit before any mainnet use. A model that is not checked is not a model, it is a guess.
  • Not audited, not advice. These are educational examples. They are not financial, legal or tax advice, and not a licensed payment or banking product. Payments, stablecoins and acquiring are regulated activities in most countries.
  • Security. Never paste a private key into a website. Do not install the npm package @wen-community/wen-new-standard: npm removed it as malicious. Build from official sources.
